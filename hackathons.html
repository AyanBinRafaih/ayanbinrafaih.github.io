Competition September 2026

AI Chessathon 2026

Sponsored by

An online chess engine competition in which each team submits a Python engine (one get_move function on a 120 s clock), which plays a rated ladder and then a Swiss qualifier for the London final.

What I built (solo)

  • Search: alpha-beta over magic bitboards, compiled with numba.
  • Evaluation: a HalfKP NNUE, trained from scratch on 260M Lichess positions.
  • Inference: int16 weights, updated incrementally after each move.

Placed 41st of 465 on the leaderboard (Elo 2344) and 31st of 334 in the Swiss qualifier, and qualified for the London final at Encode Club.
