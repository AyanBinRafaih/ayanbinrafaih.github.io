About

Hello! I'm Ayan Bin Rafaih, an undergraduate at UCL pursuing a Masters of Engineering (MEng) in Mathematics and Computer Science. My work sits at the intersection of physics, low-latency computational optimisations, mechanistic interpretability in LLMs and agentic AI systems.

I have worked with Dr. Zachary Murray at Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics and Université Côte d'Azur. Alongside that, I was at UCL Hawkes Institute (Prof. Evangelos Mazomenos), working on robotic surgical skill classification using Bayesian-optimisation-based learning methods and acceleration-based kinematics features.

Previously, I won a honourable mention at the 2nd International Olympiad of Artificial Intelligence (IOAI 2025) and was selected as a national team member for the 25th Asian Physics Olympiad (APhO 2025). I also won 2 distinguished honourable mention awards at the Stanford University Mathematics Tournament (SMT 2024, 2025), and earned 6 Outstanding Cambridge Learner Award (OCLA 2023, 2024, 2025) distinctions.

Outside of academics, I'm into kart-racing and hiking. I also enjoy playing tennis and listening to music.

Research interests: Mechanistic Interpretability · Agentic AI Systems · Large Language Models · Low-Latency Computing · Computational Physics · Bayesian Optimisation · Surgical Robotics · Computational Astrophysics . AI4Science
